Design consequences, binding:
1. The scarce resource is the real upstream budget (ViaggiaTreno via the
   real-data epic). Scarcity must be genuine — no inflating budgets to make
   the demo look good.
2. "Making the principle observable" is an acceptance dimension: viewers
   should SEE the economics — upstream req rate, peer count, share of
   samples received via peers vs own fetches, freshness. The status HUD
   graduates from debug aid to demo surface; plan a child for it.
3. Design question to develop, not assume: WHERE the global (cross-viewer)
   budget is enforced — client-side per-tab gates alone cannot bound total
   upstream load across N tabs; a proxy-side global choke point may be the
   honest enforcement of the contract. Cost both.
